The Game Master's line is a real turn, told by the AI on a phone, offline.

You roll every die

Swipe the d20 right in the story and watch it tumble, roll your own die and enter the number, or roll a Pixels smart die. The game never rolls for you, and every result is saved before the story is told.

Rules run by the game, not the AI

Every hit, price, door and odds is decided by the game's own rules. The AI can't fudge a roll or forget your gold, and what you see on screen is never taken back.

Faces, names and voices

Everyone who speaks shows their face and name, and talks in a voice of their own. Tap a name to see who they are. Out of ideas? The ideas button offers moves that matter, with their odds in plain words.

A story that lives

A world that moves, even when you don't.

The story is the game. Quiet moments matter as much as battles: a stranger's memory, a rival's taunt, a town that remembers what you did.

People with their own lives

Everyone wants something, fears something and is tied to someone. Many keep a secret, and there are always three ways to uncover it. Talk to people and they answer from what they know, think and remember about you.

Factions that act every day

Guilds, gangs, courts and rival bakeries pursue their own goals day by day. You see it in the world: a place changes hands, prices rise, a road closes, someone goes missing.

Threats you can see coming

Dangers grow while you are away, and each step is foreshadowed before it lands, so you always have a chance to act. Ignore them and the world changes for good.

Your deeds come back

The smuggler you spared returns. People quote what you said. Places carry the marks of what happened there. Every event has a cause, and the Chronicle keeps the record.

Paced like a great table

Tension builds, peaks and gives way to rest. Every telling ends on something still happening, and a failure always opens a new path instead of a dead end.

Never the same campaign twice

Each campaign gets its own mix of people, places, quests and twists, drawn to be different from the campaigns you have already played, with tired story clichés kept out.

Nine kinds of scenes

Every conflict is a scene in the story.

When things come to a head, the scene opens on its own board: the stakes in story terms, two tracks you race against, honest odds before you roll, and every move told as story. Each kind plays differently, and your world picks the ones that fit it.

FIGHTSee what your foes intend, choose your ground, and land the blow that turns it.
CHASERun or pursue through a route of obstacles, shortcuts and close calls.
INVESTIGATEGather clues, connect them and name the truth. Every answer has more than one way in.
INFILTRATESlip past watchers, read the rooms and get out before the alarm.
OUTWITWin an argument, a bargain or a bluff by finding what moves them.
PERFORMWin over a crowd, a court or a critic, beat by beat.
SURVIVEWeather the storm, the cold or the long road with what you carry.
BUILDMake something worth making: a shop, a ship, a cake for a queen.
COMPETERace, duel or play for the title against rivals who learn.

These hands-on moments also give the phone time to cool between tellings, so long sessions stay smooth.

Everything a campaign needs

A whole tabletop campaign on one phone.

A hero who changes

Your hero's picture changes as they do: new armor and weapons, scars earned the hard way, the years passing, the shift from hero to rogue to villain. Tilt it and it moves with a little depth. A gallery keeps every look.

A map drawn for your world

Parchment, sea chart, city plan, star chart or storybook, in the style of your world. Fog lifts as you explore. Tap a place to travel there, follow your quest's flag, and mark the land with your own notes. Ask it "take me to the mill".

Loot worth finding

Gear that changes your rolls, from common to legendary, named in the world's own words. Your pack lives on your hero's page: compare, equip, use, give.

The Chronicle and Codex

Your story as a journal, plus every person, place and strange word you've met, with what you know and where you learned it. Ask the Game Master a question any time without spending a turn.

Hear it, speak it

Every character speaks in a voice of their own. Speak your moves or type them, whichever you like.

Your story, your limits

Choose topics you never want to see, or want kept off-screen, and pick your storyteller: grim, wry, pulpy, cozy or lyrical. Want a passage told differently? Tell it again.

Play together

Friends on the same Wi-Fi or your hotspot join your campaign from their own phones, with no AI download of their own. Plan rounds together, and everyone rolls their own die.

Made for every player

Text up to twice the size, an easy-reading font, high contrast, reduced motion and screen-reader labels throughout. Every outcome is shown in words, never by color alone.

Painted by your phone PRO

On phones with an AI chip, scenes, portraits, places, maps, item icons and your own themed d20 are painted on the device, checked against the scene they show, and labeled as AI-made.

Choose your road

Hero, rogue or villain. ROGUE & VILLAIN: PRO

Save the town, play both sides, or take it over, in your world's own words: in a space western you are a Lawbringer, a Drifter or an Outlaw King; in a kittens' bakery, a Beloved Baker, a Scrappy Upstart or a Ruthless Tycoon. Your deeds shift where you stand, and your hero's face shows it. Play the villain with your own scheme and lieutenants while a rising hero comes to stop you. If your hero falls, their grave stays on the map, people remember the name, and a new hero begins a story that has never been told before, in the same world. Or retire them with honor: they stay in the world as a mentor for your next hero, or as the rival your next villain must face.
