How QuestHold AI downloads its AI models

QuestHold AI runs its Game Master on the phone. On first launch the player taps Download and begin, and the models download in a foreground service of type data sync, with a progress notification the player can see, pause or cancel.

  1. The player reads and accepts the Terms. 0:03
  2. The player taps Download and begin. 0:17
  3. The game explains the progress notification before Android asks for permission. 0:22
  4. The player allows notifications, and the download starts. 0:28
  5. The player leaves the app. The download keeps going. 0:36
  6. The notification shows the size, the percent done and the time left. 0:38

Permission: FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC (WorkManager's foreground service).

Task: downloading the AI models, started only by the player's tap.

Recorded on a Motorola razr ultra 2025, QuestHold AI 0.9.24, October 28, 2026. Other notifications are blurred for privacy.
