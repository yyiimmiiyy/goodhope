1. What QuestHold is

QuestHold is a story game. An AI Game Master runs your whole story: the world, its people, the dice and every turn. After a one-time download, the AI runs entirely on your phone. A phone that cannot run the AI (one with less than 6 GB of memory) can still play as a guest at a friend's table: the AI then runs on the host's phone, and nothing is downloaded to the guest's phone. QuestHold has no accounts and no servers of its own, and nothing you type or say is sent to us. If you play co-op, the phones at your table send each other the game over your own Wi-Fi or hotspot (the Privacy Policy says exactly what).

9. Free play, the trial and Pro

  • Free: up to 100 turns a day. A turn is one action you take that the Game Master answers; creating a world and character, menus, maps, dice rolls, Ask the GM, recaps and the Game Master's own retries do not count, and an action you take back with Undo is given back. If you reach the limit partway through a scene, you get up to 5 more turns to finish it (up to 10 during a fight). The count runs over a rolling 24 hours and is kept on your phone. Free play continues one world of your own at a time. Your other worlds stay saved on your phone and you can read them, but only Pro continues them. Nothing is deleted.
  • Trial: for the first 7 days after you install QuestHold, everything in Pro is unlocked.

Pro removes the daily turn limit and unlocks the Pro features listed in the app. If a parent supervises your Google account, Google Play may ask them to approve the purchase first; on an iPhone, Apple's Ask to Buy may ask a parent in the same way. Pro unlocks once they approve it.
  • Payment: purchases go through Google Play, or through the App Store on an iPhone. We never see or store your card details.
  • Refunds: refunds follow Google Play's refund policies; ask Google Play for one. On an iPhone, refunds are Apple's: ask Apple for one at reportaproblem.apple.com. A refunded purchase ends Pro.
  • Restoring Pro: Pro belongs to the Google account you bought it with. Install QuestHold on a phone signed in to that account to get it back. On an iPhone, Pro belongs to the Apple Account you bought it with: install QuestHold on any iPhone or iPad signed in to that account to get it back.

10. Your worlds live on your phone

Your worlds are stored on your phone, encrypted, and stay there unless you turn on Cloud save: then QuestHold copies each world you play to your own Google account, as a saved game in Google Play Games (Privacy Policy, section 7). We never have a copy and cannot recover them. Uninstalling QuestHold, clearing its storage, resetting the phone, or a reset of the phone's secure key store deletes the worlds on the phone or makes them unreadable; only a copy you saved with Cloud save, or in a backup file (Settings, Back up and restore), can be brought back. Please keep this in mind before you do any of those.

On an iPhone there is no Cloud save, and the key that locks your worlds is kept in the iPhone's Keychain. Deleting QuestHold deletes the worlds, and erasing the iPhone, or moving to a new iPhone without its backup, makes them unreadable. A backup file made in QuestHold can always bring them back.
