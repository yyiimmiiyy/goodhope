We are an independent software studio. We build AI apps that run on the device in your hand, mobile games, and developer tools powered by Claude.

What we work on

On-device AI companions

Mobile apps that pair a local language model with real-world data, built for outdoor and hobby pursuits where a signal is not guaranteed.

Mobile games

A portfolio of casual games across several genres, built on a shared engine so that each new title starts from proven foundations.

Applied machine learning

Custom-trained image classifiers and retrieval pipelines that draw on public environmental and reference data to ground what our apps say.

Developer tools

Hindsight, a free pull request reviewer powered by Claude that learns from a team's past mistakes and checks every later change against them. About Hindsight

How we build

  • Private by defaultWherever we can, the model and your data stay on your device rather than on our servers.
  • Useful offlineOur apps are designed to keep working when the network does not.
  • Small team, shared foundationsWe invest in common engines and pipelines so a small studio can ship and maintain many products.
